How the pacing works

The blank stare is the thing this is built around. Sitting in it indefinitely teaches him that going still makes demands stop — but cutting in too early teaches him that waiting produces the answer. So there are two stages: at the halfway mark it repeats the word without giving anything away, and only at the full wait does it outline the right card. Set that wait long enough that he usually beats it, and stretch it as he gets faster.

Behind that, every answer moves a hidden checkout-risk score: fast and right brings it down, slow answers and no-responses push it up. As it climbs the app makes things easier before he notices — drops in cards he already owns to rebuild momentum, then narrows four choices to three to two. Fill the dot board and he gets the thing he'd otherwise be escaping to. If the risk stays high after two rewards, it ends the session on a card he knows, so the last thing that happens is a win. The bar at the top is for you, not him.
